01 / Begin

From an unfinished idea to an approved starting structure

You do not need to understand the template files or complete a long questionnaire. The AI reads the detailed protocol and leads the conversation using product, delivery, architecture, QA, and risk perspectives. A short description of your idea is enough to start.

Step 01

Use a new repository

Copy this framework into an empty or nearly empty repository intended for a new project.

Step 02

Open it with your AI tool

The tool must be able to read the repository and, after your approval, create or update files.

Step 03

Send one instruction

Copy the instruction below, add your idea, and let the AI guide the next part of the discussion.

Current scope This version is for greenfield projects only. It does not yet migrate, import, or restructure an existing software project.

Instruction for your AI

Add your idea after the final sentence. It can be incomplete.

02 / The process

What happens after you describe the idea

Initialization is a conversation with a visible approval boundary. The AI organizes the information, supplies professional perspectives you may not know to request, and keeps its own assumptions separate from project facts.

  1. Confirm the starting condition The AI checks that the repository is suitable for a new project.

  2. Understand the idea It extracts what you already said and asks only the next useful questions about users, value, scope, evidence, risk, runtime, and collaboration.

  3. Shape the first delivery Together you define the outcome, non-goals, system and data boundaries, risks, and acceptance evidence before implementation begins.

  4. Prepare the proposal The AI explains the first product scope, learning priorities, authority boundaries, repository categories, governance needs, test strategy, intended files, and verification plan.

  5. Wait for your decision You may approve, correct, narrow, reject, defer, or continue the discussion. Silence is not approval.

  6. Create and verify After approval, the AI creates only the agreed structure, validates it, and reports remaining unknowns and limitations. It checks the result against the original idea and stops for discussion if reality no longer matches the agreement.

03 / Your role

You carry the decisions. AI helps you see which decisions matter.

You are not expected to arrive as an expert Product Owner, project manager, architect, or QA lead. AI applies those perspectives throughout the project. It uses Agile feedback, Lean learning, risk management, architecture, testing, and operational thinking to explain choices and bring material decisions back to you.

The human is the Product Owner

The framework helps you apply product, project, architecture, QA, and operational perspectives without requiring you to know every professional question in advance or supervise every implementation detail.

You retain authority over

  • the problem, intended outcome, and success evidence
  • priority, scope, and acceptable risk
  • AI roles, permissions, and external actions
  • production release and irreversible decisions
  • activation of governance that changes how the project is controlled
The partnership continues For each non-trivial feature, AI reconnects the request to the original idea, discusses scope and boundaries, agrees on test evidence before coding, and checks the result back against the agreement. A material discrepancy pauses the work for your decision.

04 / Where we start

The human and AI enter through different doors

The previous sections explain what will happen. This is where the work begins. The human starts with index.html to understand the process and make the important decisions. The AI starts with START_HERE.md to guide initialization. AGENTS.md controls the partnership and roles. PROJECT_WORKFLOW.md controls how project work is delivered.

Human entrance

index.html

Read this page first. It shows what to expect, what remains under human authority, and how to begin the conversation with AI.

AI starting entrance

START_HERE.md

The one-time initialization protocol. It tells the AI how to conduct the first conversation, configure the project rules, prepare the proposal, and wait for approval.

AI continuing rules

AGENTS.md

A compact team charter defining authority, role boundaries, permissions, peer review, concurrent writers, and when the AI must return a decision to the human.

AI project method

PROJECT_WORKFLOW.md

The canonical method throughout the project for shaping features, managing risk, defining boundaries and tests, organizing the repository, tracking work, verifying completion, and checking results against the original idea.

05 / After approval

What the AI may create for your project

The exact set depends on the project. Each artifact must answer a distinct question. Unnecessary empty documents are not a sign of completeness.

Project profile

Approved facts, unresolved questions, initialization state, and active governance.

Product brief

The problem, intended users, first value, scope, assumptions, and success evidence.

Repository and current state

Where code, documents, tests, tools, data, and runtime output belong, and what is true about the system now.

Feature and test agreement

The outcome, non-goals, affected boundaries, risks, acceptance evidence, and test strategy.

Current work

The unfinished work that is active now, with priority and visible blockers.

Decisions, history, and overview

Why durable decisions were made, what was completed, and a readable HTML view of current truth.

06 / Grow when needed

The complete framework can exist without every rule being active

Governance is activated by real changes in collaboration, operation, or risk. When a trigger appears, the AI explains the need, previews the change, and requests approval.

A reviewer joins

Activate role separation, review output, and finding resolution.

A second writer joins

Define ownership, branches or worktrees, integration, and shared-file boundaries.

Production data appears

Add test isolation, read-only boundaries, backup, recovery, and data ownership.

Work becomes unattended

Add idempotency, locks, retries, monitoring, and human takeover conditions.

Several long efforts compete

Separate the roadmap, current work list, and detailed feature plans.

Impact or risk increases

Require stronger evidence, explicit authority, independent checks, and recovery.

07 / Know the limits

This is an early working framework, not an automatic project generator

  • It currently supports new projects only.
  • The AI performs the protocol. A separate initialization service is not required.
  • Missing tools and failed checks must be reported honestly.
  • Passwords, API keys, and private credentials must not enter project documents.
  • Generated HTML is a human view, not an independent source of project truth.
  • The framework is open to testing, criticism, and improvement.
